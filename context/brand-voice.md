# ABAA Project Tone & Voice

## Brand Voice

### Primary Characteristics
- **Authoritative** - Expert perspective on healthcare and systems
- **Thoughtful** - Reflective and philosophical
- **Accessible** - Clear communication without jargon
- **Inspirational** - Motivating call-to-action
- **Professional** - Polished and credible

## Writing Guidelines

### Headlines
- Clear and benefit-focused
- Conversational yet professional
- Avoid hype, emphasize substance

### Body Copy
- Active voice preferred
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Real examples and anecdotes
- Storytelling approach

### CTAs (Calls-to-Action)
- Action-oriented verbs
- Value proposition clear
- Low friction (email signup)

## Visual Communication

- Clean, modern aesthetic
- Plenty of white space
- High-quality imagery
- Consistent typography
- Professional color palette

## Tone Examples

❌ **Avoid:**
- "ABAA is the best expert ever!"
- Marketing hyperbole
- Overly casual language
- Technical jargon

✅ **Use:**
- "Explore how systems thinking transforms healthcare"
- Genuine stories and experiences
- Professional but warm language
- Concepts explained clearly

## Content Personality

- Thoughtful and contemplative
- Action-oriented
- Inclusive and welcoming
- Evidence-based claims
- Humble expertise
